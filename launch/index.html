Launch note · October 11, 2026

A second look
before send.

The Synapse Guard public browser demo is available now. It checks a narrow set of commitments against evidence you deliberately supply.

A delivery date can drift while a draft still says “Friday.” A proposed meeting can overlap an existing event. A draft can refer to a file that is not actually attached. Those are the moments this checker is built to help you review.

Try the workflow

The demo can load fictional examples or compare your draft with your own supplied evidence. Its output includes possible conflicts, cited records when a finding is supported, and coverage for tickets, calendar, and attachments. Everything in this demo is evaluated in browser memory.

The useful answer can be “incomplete”

During a staging check with a real Jira record, an unmapped provider status exposed a false clean result. We repaired it. The checker now keeps unknown ticket statuses incomplete instead of implying that a completion claim is supported. That is part of the product contract.

What is still in development

The hosted integration and saved workspace evidence are in private staging. The public demo has no automatic source retrieval, background synchronization, billing, or team administration. We have not published an accuracy benchmark or a customer-count claim.

Help test the next version

We are seeking a small number of design partners who regularly make delivery or meeting commitments. You can try the local demo first and then tell us where the workflow is useful or confusing. Share only synthetic or redacted examples in the public community.
